# openkit-brushmaker-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy brushmaker (paint/utility brush) workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-bristle-bundle` — 132 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); three twine-tied hog/boar bristle knots standing on a small oak sorting board, sized for paint brushes (not broom twigs)
- `prop-ferrule-blank-brm1` — 156 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); low oak tray with six graded tin and brass brush ferrule blanks (slightly tapered tubes)
- `prop-handle-blank-brm1` — 132 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); three short turned beech paint-brush handle blanks (22/19/16 cm) with tenon ends, laid on a canvas cloth; not broomsticks
- `prop-setting-pitch-pot` — 128 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); small iron pot of brushmaker setting pitch on a fired tile, with a stirring/setting stick, used to cement bristle knots into ferrules
