# openkit-pouch-maker-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy pouch/bag-maker workshop blanks. These are not finished pouches or inventory icons. Each is <=210 tris, 1 m units, Z-up in Blender and Y-up in FBX. FBX files carry material slots only. Optional BaseColor PNGs are in tex/.

## Pivots
- prop-pouch-panel-blank: laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 14x12 cm unfinished undyed leather pouch panel cutout lying flat, chalk mouth + seam lines, untrimmed offcut tab +X
- prop-drawstring-cord-coil: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 8 cm flat coil of 3 mm waxed drawstring cord, loose whipped end to +X/-Y, small beeswax cake on top
- prop-flap-blank: laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 11x10 cm unfinished leather pouch flap blank lying flat, scored fold crease near +Y hinge edge, chalk-marked uncut toggle slit near rounded -Y tip
- prop-rivet-eyelet-tray: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 16x10 cm shallow oak tray with centre divider, copper pouch rivets left bay, brass eyelets right bay
