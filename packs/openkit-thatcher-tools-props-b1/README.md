# openkit-thatcher-tools-props-b1

Four procedural (Blender bpy) thatcher workshop tools. 1 m units, Z-up in Blender / Y-up FBX, slots-only materials; flat tint BaseColor PNGs in tex/.

## Pivots
- `prop-spar-bundle` (132 tris, [0.7, 0.09, 0.06] m): bottom_centre (0,0,0) on floor; lying 3-tier bundle of nine 0.70 m split hazel spars along X, two ties at x=+/-0.2 (loose straight spars, not twisted/pointed staples)
- `prop-thatching-needle` (50 tris, [1.1, 0.032, 0.031] m): bottom_centre of grip on floor (bbox min z=0); laid flat along X, 0.9 m iron needle with eye near point (+X), wooden grip at -X
- `prop-legget-beetle` (84 tris, [0.516, 0.22, 0.232] m): bottom_centre of board on floor; ribbed 0.26x0.22 m wooden legget face down, handle angled up toward +X
- `prop-sway-rod-stack` (164 tris, [1.8, 0.18, 0.058] m): bottom_centre (0,0,0) on floor; 2-tier stack of seven 1.8 m round hazel sway rods along X on two oak chocks

Textures: T_Iron, T_Wood_Dark and T_Wood_Oak are byte-identical to shared Open Kit pack copies; Unity may dedupe those three.
