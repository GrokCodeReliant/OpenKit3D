# openkit-comb-maker-props-b1
Comb-maker workshop props. 1 m units, Z-up in Blender, FBX slots-only.

## Pivots
- prop-comb-blank-stack: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 5 slightly skewed 0.11x0.05x0.005 m untoothed blanks, alternating boxwood/horn plates, stacked flush (no gaps) (60 tris)
- prop-tooth-saw-cbm1: floor under handle/spine joint (0,0,0) offset; laid along X on its edge-down blade + handle; 0.21 m brass-backed fine tooth saw (36 tris)
- prop-comb-clamp-board: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.2x0.09 m oak jig board, horn blank pinned under clamp bar by 2 pegs, scribed tooth marks (148 tris)
- prop-polishing-cloth-roll: bottom_centre (0,0,0)-ish; 0.12 m felt/linen roll along X with twine band, tail unrolled flat toward -Y (84 tris)
