# openkit-block-plane-props-b1
CC0 Open Kit medieval-fantasy block plane blanks: body, iron, wedge, lever-cap. Units 1 m, pivot bottom centre. 56 tris total.

- `prop-block-plane-body-bp1` - 20 tris - Compact wooden block-plane body blank: solid 50x150x45 mm rectangular prism with 8 mm top chamfers along both long edges and a flat sole; no mouth/throat cut.
- `prop-block-plane-iron-bp1` - 12 tris - Short flat steel block-plane iron blank: plain 36 mm wide x 85 mm long x 3 mm thick bar lying flat, no bevel ground.
- `prop-block-plane-wedge-bp1` - 8 tris - Wooden wedge blank: right-triangle prism 30 mm wide x 50 mm long, rising to 15 mm at the back end, flat underside.
- `prop-block-plane-cap-bp1` - 16 tris - Small steel lever-cap blank plate: 34 mm wide x 50 mm long x 5 mm thick, front top edge chamfered down to 2 mm, flat underside to sit on the iron.
