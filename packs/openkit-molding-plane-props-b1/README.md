# openkit-molding-plane-props-b1
CC0 Open Kit medieval-fantasy molding plane blanks: body, iron, wedge, side fence. Units 1 m, pivot bottom centre. 60 tris total.

- `prop-molding-plane-body-mp1` - 24 tris - Wooden molding-plane body blank: tall narrow 25x250x80 mm rectangular prism with one V-chamfered cove groove (10 mm wide x 6 mm deep) running the full length of the sole, centred 3 mm toward +x; no mouth/throat or iron slot cut.
- `prop-molding-plane-iron-mp1` - 16 tris - Profiled steel molding-plane iron blank lying flat with long axis on X: 90 mm long x 25 mm wide x 4 mm thick bar whose cutting end comes to a shallow convex V point (7 mm proud) matching the body cove; no bevel ground.
- `prop-molding-plane-wedge-mp1` - 8 tris - Wooden wedge blank: right-triangle prism 22 mm wide x 80 mm long, rising to 16 mm at the back end, flat underside.
- `prop-molding-plane-fence-mp1` - 12 tris - Thin wooden side fence blank: plain flat board 6 mm thick x 220 mm long x 30 mm tall standing on edge, to bolt along the body side; no bolt holes cut.
