# openkit-bellfounder-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy bell-founder workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-bell-blank-bf1` — 152 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 40 cm rough-cast bronze bell blank, hollow shell with un-trimmed crown stub and casting flash seam; no ornament, untuned
- `prop-cope-drag-set` — 208 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); loam bell mould pair side by side: loam core (drag) on wooden board, and the hollow cope shell with iron lift ears and pour cup. Round loam bell mould, NOT a square sand flask
- `prop-tuning-lathe-blank` — 144 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 80 cm low bench-top bell tuning lathe fixture: oak bed, headstock, iron spindle with faceplate, tool rest with flat scraper laid on it; no bell mounted
- `prop-loam-trowel-blank` — 44 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 32 cm loam-moulding trowel: tapered iron leaf blade, cranked tang, ash handle; for sweeping loam on bell moulds (not a plasterer's square float)
