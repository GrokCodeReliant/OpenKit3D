# openkit-basket-weaver-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy basket-weaver workshop props (materials, tools, WIP) for Open Kit. All <=210 tris, 1 m units, slots-only FBX; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/. PG.

## Pivots
- prop-willow-bundle: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under bundle, lying along X; 1.2 m bundle of 7 peeled willow withies, staggered ends, two twine ties
- prop-basket-unfinished: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under base board; 0.36 m WIP basket: octagonal wooden base, only a 7 cm woven band, 8 bare splayed stakes standing up, one loose weaver; no rim, no handle, no contents
- prop-bodkin-awl-bw1: bottom_centre (0,0,0) lying flat along X; 0.17 m tapered bone basket-maker's bodkin with short turned wood handle (blunt-pointed, for opening the weave; not a cobbler awl)
- prop-soaking-tub-small: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under tub base; 0.44 m-diameter low staved soaking tub (0.24 m tall body) with 2 iron hoops, lug handles, water and two withies soaking at an angle; not a vat/crock/barrel

Renames: none (bodkin uses -bw1 suffix by design).
Rebuild: blender -b -P src/build.py
