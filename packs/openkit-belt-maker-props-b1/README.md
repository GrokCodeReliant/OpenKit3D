# openkit-belt-maker-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy belt/strap-maker workshop blanks. These are not finished belts or UI icons. Each is <=210 tris, 1 m units, Z-up in Blender and Y-up in FBX. FBX files carry material slots only. Optional BaseColor PNGs are in tex/.

## Pivots
- prop-strap-blank-roll: bottom_centre (0,0,0); flat-wound 15 cm coil of 3.8 cm wide undyed belt strap lying flat, rough square-cut tail out to +X/-Y, cord tie, chalk line
- prop-buckle-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 5x5 cm rough-cast buckle frame blank lying flat, centre bar but no tongue, sprue stub +X, flash fin on +Y edge
- prop-tip-chape-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 6.5 cm folded brass belt-end (strap tip) chape blank lying flat, open strap slot -X, unfiled rounded tip +X with cut-off nub, rivet holes undrilled
- prop-punch-block-bt1: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 30 cm end-grain oak punching stump (8-seg), leather strap offcut and punch-mark scars on top, lead pad
