# Look and limits

- Stone blocks and slabs have chamfered edges like the kit's own modules. The iron parts, the stocks' feet, uprights, braces and yoke boards, and the trapdoor planks still have hard edges; at close range they look plain.
- The slabs have no chips or wear and only 2–6 mm of height variation, so they look emptier than the kit's more irregular stone floor.
- The grate covers a flat, dark bed 4.5 cm below the floor. From a top-down camera it reads as a dark square rather than a hole. There is no water.
- The trapdoor is closed, with no separate leaf or hinge pivot. It cannot open without remodelling. The pull ring is only 10 cm across and is easy to miss from a distant camera.
- The cell front has no door; a cell needs another piece for its entrance. Its 6-sided bars look faceted up close. Its cap course uses the stone slot (Secondary); the kit's stone wall cap uses Accent, so a theme palette may colour the two caps differently.
- The bar wall's collision is one thin solid panel over the bars. Arrows, spells and line-of-sight rays are blocked unless you set up something finer.
- On the stocks, the frame and yoke boards are close in tone and do not separate as clearly as intended. The hardware is on the front only. The subject is a punishment device, which may be too grim for some scenes.
- All observations above come from Blender previews only. Nothing has been checked under game-engine lighting, against a character, or with a camera-fade shader.
