# openkit-cutler-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy cutler/knife-maker workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-blade-blank-cu1` — 36 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 28 cm rough-forged knife blank: unground blade slab with crude taper, flat full tang, forge-scale bolster lump; no edge, no handle
- `prop-grindstone-small-cu1` — 148 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 75 cm bench/hand-crank cutler's grindstone: 40 cm sandstone wheel on iron axle in oak A-frame with sill feet and crank
- `prop-handle-scale-blank` — 96 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 20 cm shallow tray holding two pairs of rough-sawn wooden handle scales plus one pale bone scale; undrilled
- `prop-rivet-tray-cu1` — 192 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 18 cm turned wooden dish of cutler's handle rivets (brass heads) with a washer stack; not a leather eyelet tray
