# openkit-silversmith-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy silversmith workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX). Workshop blanks only; no finished jewelry.

- `prop-wire-draw-plate-ss1` — 108 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid 18 cm hardened steel draw plate blank, single row of 8 graduated holes; distinct from pin-maker prop-wire-draw-plate (renamed -ss1)
- `prop-chasing-hammer-ss1` — 124 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid chasing hammer: broad flat face + small ball peen, thin ash handle with bulb grip; distinct from blacksmith/cutler hammers
- `prop-pitch-bowl-ss1` — 198 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); round-bottom cast iron chasing bowl full of pitch, seated on leather ring, bare sheet blank set in pitch; distinct from brushmaker setting-pitch-pot
- `prop-scrap-silver-tray` — 176 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); ash board tray of sheet silver offcuts, wire snips and a filings heap for remelt; no finished pieces; distinct from pewterer/smith scrap trays
