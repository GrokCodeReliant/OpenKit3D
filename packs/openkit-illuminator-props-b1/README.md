# openkit-illuminator-props-b1

Four low-poly manuscript-illumination props (pigments and tools). Procedurally built in Blender 5.2.1. 1 m units, pivot bottom-centre at origin.

| Asset | Tris | Dims m (XYZ) | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| prop-gold-size-pot | 128 | 0.099 x 0.072 x 0.066 | small glazed clay pot of sticky gold size (mordant for laying leaf) with a short brush resting across the rim |
| prop-vermilion-cake | 56 | 0.15 x 0.11 x 0.026 | single bright red vermilion/minium pigment cake with a broken chip and crumb on an irregular parchment scrap |
| prop-squirrel-hair-brush | 72 | 0.19 x 0.006 x 0.007 | fine illuminator's brush: long slender wooden handle, quill ferrule and needle-pointed squirrel-hair tip |
| prop-pounce-bag | 146 | 0.084 x 0.084 x 0.095 | small linen bag of pounce powder, gathered at the neck by a drawstring with a dangling tie end |

Folders: `fbx/` models (material slots only), `tex/` flat BaseColor PNGs.
