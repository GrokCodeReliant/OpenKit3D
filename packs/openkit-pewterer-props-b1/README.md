# openkit-pewterer-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy pewterer workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-pewter-ingot-tray` — 204 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 28x18 cm ash board tray of six small trapezoid pewter ingots/pigs (raw stock; distinct from foundry/blacksmith iron and scrap trays)
- `prop-spoon-mold-blank` — 96 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid open two-part soapstone spoon casting mold blank (bowl cavity + handle channel + pour gate), no spoon cast; distinct from spoon-carver finished wooden spoons and foundry flask-mold-half
- `prop-ladle-pewter-blank` — 100 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid 30 cm unfinished cast pewter table ladle with sprue stub still attached, unpolished; distinct from foundry prop-ladle-pour-blank (crucible pour ladle)
- `prop-scrap-pewter-tray` — 208 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); board tray of pewter offcuts, sprue sticks and two runner buttons for remelt; distinct from smith prop-scrap-iron-tray
