# openkit-mason-props-b1

4 CC0 medieval stonemason props, procedural Blender bpy (src/build.py). 1 m units, slots-only FBX.

## Pivots
- **prop-mallet-stone** (104 tris, [0.435, 0.242, 0.15] m): bottom_centre (0,0,0) on floor/bench; laid flat: 0.15 m dia x 0.22 m barrel-head beechwood mallet with iron face hoops, 0.30 m handle along +X, axis +Z
- **prop-chisel-point-ms1** (66 tris, [0.41, 0.044, 0.038] m): bottom_centre (0,0,0) on bench; laid flat along X: 0.13 m wood handle + ferrule + 0.27 m iron shank tapering to a pyramid-ish point, axis +Z
- **prop-ashlar-block** (24 tris, [0.5, 0.3, 0.305] m): bottom_centre (0,0,0) on floor; 0.50x0.30x0.30 m dressed limestone ashlar with slightly raised tooled top panel, axis +Z
- **prop-plumb-bob** (108 tris, [0.24, 0.102, 0.091] m): bottom_centre (0,0,0) on bench/floor; resting pose: 0.08 m lead teardrop bob upright with slack cord to a small wood winder handle, axis +Z

Materials are named slots only; tex/ holds optional flat BaseColor PNGs.
