# openkit-foundry-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy foundry / casting workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-crucible-blank-fd1` — 152 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 22 cm clay-graphite crucible blank, tapered pot with thick base, open mouth and small pour lip; empty, unfired-look
- `prop-ladle-pour-blank` — 136 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); ~1.25 m two-man pouring ladle blank resting level: wrought-iron bowl, long shank, cross carry bar, wooden end grip, prop foot; empty
- `prop-flask-mold-half` — 140 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 43 cm square wooden sand-flask half (drag) packed with moulding sand, iron lugs and alignment pins, dark pattern impression and sprue mark
- `prop-slag-tray` — 168 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 50 cm iron scrap tray with handles holding cooled slag lumps and a cut-off sprue/runner piece for remelt
