# openkit-helm-maker-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy helm-maker workshop blanks & stake. <=120 tris each, 1 m units, Z-up Blender / Y-up FBX, slots-only FBX; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/.

## Pivots
- prop-skull-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.24 m rough hammered iron dome/skull blank resting rim-down, untrimmed flat rim lip, no holes/visor/fittings
- prop-brow-band-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0) = arc centre on floor; 3.5 cm x 4 mm iron strip blank bent ~220 deg open arc standing on edge, opening toward -X, plain untrimmed (no rivets/holes)
- prop-rivet-anvil-hm1: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.2 m oak stump with tapered iron post stake ending in 8.4 cm mushroom/ball head for doming & riveting helm skulls
- prop-lining-pad-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.27 x 0.2 m oval padded leather lining blank lying flat, single linen tacking line along X, untrimmed (no lacing/straps)
