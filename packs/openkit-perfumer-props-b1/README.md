# openkit-perfumer-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy perfumer / distiller workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-still-blank-pf1` — 132 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 20 cm small perfumer's still blank: unglazed clay pot with plain copper head cap and straight spout stub, no coil/receiver (distinct from alchemy prop-alembic-simple)
- `prop-rose-petal-tray` — 168 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 26x18 cm board tray of dried rose petals as flower stock (loose bed plus scattered petals; distinct from dyer/apothecary herb stock)
- `prop-perfume-vial-blank` — 180 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); board tray holding four unstoppered squat perfume vial blanks, alternating green glass and bisque clay (distinct from potion-bottle and jar-stopper-tray)
- `prop-gum-tray-pf1` — 180 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); board tray of six amber gum/resin lumps (frankincense/benzoin-style perfume stock; distinct from oak-gall and pigment-cake trays)
