# openkit-matmaker-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy rush-mat maker workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-rush-bundle-mt1` — 120 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 1.1 m sorted, dried rush bundle lying flat, two straw ties, butt-to-tip graded for mat plaiting (flat bundle, not a rushlight pith or thatch yealm)
- `prop-braid-blank` — 180 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); ~1 m unfinished flat rush plait strip (chevron plait) with three loose working strands at one end; to be sewn into a mat
- `prop-needle-mat-mt1` — 44 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 34 cm flat iron mat-sewing needle/bodkin with large eye and wooden grip, for stitching plait strips together (distinct from thatching, sail and net needles)
- `prop-edge-cord-coil` — 180 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); flat coil of twisted rush edge-binding cord with loose tail, for whipping mat borders
