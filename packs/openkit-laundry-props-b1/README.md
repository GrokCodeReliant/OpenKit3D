# openkit-laundry-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy laundry/washhouse props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-washboard-blank` — 132 tris — standing / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 60 cm all-wood washboard blank: two stiles as feet, top/bottom rails, 7 horizontal rubbing ribs; lay flat by rotating 90 deg X
- `prop-lye-tub-small` — 172 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 48 cm open coopered wash/lye (bucking) tub: tapered staves, iron hoop, low drain bung for lye run-off, two lug handles; not a ceramic lye pot or wicker soaking tub
- `prop-clothes-prop-blank` — 36 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 1.75 m forked clothes prop (drying-line pole) blank, Y-fork at +X end; stand it by rotating 90 deg Y
- `prop-iron-flat-blank` — 48 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 15 cm sadiron blank: tapered iron sole, two cast posts, wooden grip; sole down
