# openkit-quill-cutter-props-b1
CC0 medieval-fantasy quill-cutter workshop props: cutting block, nib slit gauge, scrap tray, curing sand pot.

- `prop-quill-cutting-block-qc1` - 24 tris - Flat hardwood cutting pad 160x100x25 mm with a raised 15 mm end-stop lip (33 mm total height) for bracing quills while trimming.
- `prop-nib-slit-gauge-qc1` - 28 tris - Thin bone gauge strip 90x18x3 mm with a 12 mm long, 2.4 mm wide slit notch cut into one end for checking nib slits.
- `prop-quill-scrap-tray-qc1` - 36 tris - Shallow open ash-wood tray 140x100x22 mm, 7 mm walls, 6 mm floor, holding two low wedge-shaped quill shavings.
- `prop-curing-sand-pot-qc1` - 48 tris - Small six-sided bellied glazed clay pot 100 mm wide, 75 mm tall, open mouth filled with a slightly mounded fine sand surface for curing cut nibs; no lid, no perforations.

Units 1 m, pivot bottom centre. FBX has material slots only; BaseColor PNGs in `tex/`.
