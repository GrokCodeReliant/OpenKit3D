# openkit-shield-maker-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy shield-maker workshop blanks for Open Kit. All <=210 tris, 1 m units, slots-only FBX; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/.

## Pivots
- prop-blank-board-stack (72 tris): bottom_centre (0,0,0); three layers of two rough-sawn, unglued, uncut rectangular shield board planks (0.8 x 0.165 x 0.022 m), layers slightly skewed, resting on each other
- prop-boss-blank (62 tris): bottom_centre (0,0,0); raised iron dome still on its square untrimmed sheet flange, no rivet holes, no shaping of rim
- prop-rim-iron-coil (136 tris): bottom_centre (0,0,0); flat-wound clock-spring coil (2.5 turns) of 30 mm wrought iron rim binding strip standing on edge, cord tie across turns at -Y
- prop-grip-strap-blank (36 tris): bottom_centre (0,0,0) laid flat; 0.46 m uncut leather grip strap with an unshaped square ash grip bar blank resting mid-strap and a loose iron clench plate blank near +X end
