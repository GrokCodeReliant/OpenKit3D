# openkit-feltmaker-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy feltmaker workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-carding-board-blank` — 60 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid ash carding paddle blank with bare leather card-cloth bed and nail strips, no wool
- `prop-felting-mallet` — 68 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid wooden felting/fulling mallet with felt-wrapped head and cord band; hand tool, distinct from hat-block and fuller stocks
- `prop-soap-solution-bowl` — 156 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); wide earthenware bowl of warm olive-soap felting solution with a few suds; no soap bar/lye vat, distinct from laundry & soap-maker
- `prop-pattern-sheet-ft1` — 72 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); laid flat parchment felting pattern template with inked panel outlines and a weight stone; flat, not a hat cone/blank
