# openkit-lantern-maker-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy lantern-maker / tinsmith workshop props (tools + WIP parts) for Open Kit. All <=210 tris (max 116), 1 m units, slots-only FBX; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/. PG.

## Pivots
- prop-lantern-frame-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under base plate; 0.22 m unfinished square wire lantern cage on hex tin base, one top bar missing, loose flat tin sheet beside; no glass, no roof, no bail, no flame
- prop-horn-pane-stack: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under lowest pane; 0.14 m stack of 5 translucent-amber horn panes, slightly fanned, tied with a twine band
- prop-oil-flask-tin: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under can base; 0.16 m squat tin oil filler can, angled thin spout, iron strap handle; opaque metal, not a bottle/jar
- prop-tin-snips: bottom_centre (0,0,0) lying flat; 0.29 m tinsmith snips, short crossed blades, long handles with leather grips, rivet pivot

Renames: none.
Rebuild: blender -b -P src/build.py
