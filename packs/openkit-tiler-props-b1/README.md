# openkit-tiler-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy roof-tiler workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-tile-batten-blank` — 60 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 1.2 m x 38x25 mm sawn tile batten/lath blank, three nail heads and a chalked gauge mark; loose blank, not a roof panel
- `prop-nib-tile-blank` — 48 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 16.5x26.5 cm green (unfired) pale clay nib-tile blank with pinched nib and untrimmed edge flash, on a sanded patch; reads as raw blank, not fired tile-roof-clay
- `prop-tile-hammer-tl1` — 64 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 32 cm ash-handled tiler's hammer, round striking face one side, pick point other side for holing tiles
- `prop-scoring-awl-tl1` — 52 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 21 cm tile scoring awl, 6-sided wooden handle, iron ferrule and tapered square spike

Note: M_Chalk is a flat-colour material slot (no tex map).
