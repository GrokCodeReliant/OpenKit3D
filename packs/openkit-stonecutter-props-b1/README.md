# openkit-stonecutter-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy stonecutter/quarry workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-pitching-tool-stc1` — 68 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 25 cm pitching tool: octagonal shank, broad flat blunt edge for knocking off waste, mushroomed head; not a mason point chisel
- `prop-feather-wedge-set` — 156 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 3 plug-and-feather sets (wedge between two half-round feathers) on a 20 cm board, laid for splitting stone
- `prop-banker-block-blank` — 48 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 64 cm banker (working bench) with sand bed and a rough, unsquared quarry blank skewed on top; not an ashlar
- `prop-bush-hammer-blank` — 72 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 36 cm bush hammer: square iron head with 2x2 pyramid-tooth grid face (implied), oak handle; not a mallet

Note: M_Iron_Dark and M_Sand are flat-colour material slots (no tex maps).
