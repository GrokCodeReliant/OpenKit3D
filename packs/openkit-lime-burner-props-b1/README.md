# openkit-lime-burner-props-b1 (CC0)
Lime burner / lime kiln workshop props for Open Kit. All <=210 tris, 1 m units, slots-only FBX; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/.

## Pivots
- prop-lime-rake: bottom_centre-ish (0,0,0) lying flat near pole midpoint; 2.0 m pole along X, solid 0.30 m iron draw-blade (no tines) at +X for raking burnt lime out of the draw-hole
- prop-kiln-door-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.56 m wide, 0.65 m tall unfinished stone draw-hole cover slab on sill, two iron straps + pull bar on -Y face
- prop-lime-lump-sack: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.4 m dia pale lime-dusted sack, cuff turned down, chalk-white quicklime lumps heaped and two spilled at +X
- prop-slaking-trough-small: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 0.70x0.42 m plank trough on two skids, filled with white lime putty, stirring paddle standing in it

Textures: T_Iron is byte-identical to the net-mender shared copy; Unity may point T_Iron at packs/openkit-net-mender-props-b1/tex/T_Iron_BaseColor.png to dedupe.
