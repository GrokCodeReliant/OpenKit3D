- `prop-gouge-set-tu1` — 148 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); two long-handled turning gouges (roughing + spindle) with ash handles and iron ferrules laid on a leather tool roll
- `prop-drive-center-blank` — 112 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); forged four-spur lathe drive centre with tapered shank resting on an oak block
- `prop-scrap-bowl-blank` — 112 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 26 cm rough-turned green-wood bowl blank, octagonal faceted outside, foot tenon, shallow rough hollow
