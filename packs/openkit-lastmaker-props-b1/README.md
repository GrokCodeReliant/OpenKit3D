# openkit-lastmaker-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy last-maker workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-last-blank-lm1` — 36 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 27 cm rough axe-hewn hornbeam last blank: squared-off tapered block with chop facets and bark scar, no comb/peg hole yet (unfinished; distinct from cobbler's finished prop-shoe-last)
- `prop-lasting-pincers` — 76 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 26 cm iron lasting pincers with broad gripping jaws and hammer cheek for pulling upper over last
- `prop-welt-awl-lm1` — 52 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 19 cm welt awl: pear handle, ferrule, flat blade with curved tip for welt seam (distinct from straight cobbler/stitch/punch awls)
- `prop-heel-lift-blank` — 72 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); stack of five cut sole-leather heel lifts plus a wooden top lift blank, slightly staggered, unshaped
