# openkit-scribe-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy scribe/scriptorium desk props for Open Kit. All <=210 tris (max 140), 1 m units, slots-only FBX; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/. PG.

## Pivots
- prop-writing-slope: bottom_centre (0,0,0) on table/floor; 0.48 m portable lap/table writing slope, board tilts ~14 deg down toward -Y with raised front lip, pen ledge at back; not a desk
- prop-sand-shaker: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under foot; 0.07 m waisted pewter pounce/sand pot, dished top with 5 dark hole studs
- prop-sealing-wax-stick: bottom_centre (0,0,0) of footprint on table; two 0.13 m thin hex red wax sticks lying along X plus small upright brass seal stamp at +X
- prop-parchment-sheet-stack: bottom_centre (0,0,0) under stack; 0.3x0.22 m flat stack of 7 unbound fanned parchment sheets, 2.7 cm tall, no text
