# openkit-sailmaker-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy sailmaker workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-sail-needle-set` — 196 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 32 cm oak board with felt pad holding five graduated triangular-point sail needles and a beeswax cake
- `prop-palm-thimble` — 148 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); sailmaker's palm laid flat (band axis vertical): leather hand band with thumb loop, palm pad and dimpled iron thimble plate, laced seam
- `prop-bolt-rope-coil` — 192 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); flat tarred bolt-rope coil (~26 cm) whose tail is sewn to a strip of canvas tabling with twine seizings - sail-edge rope, not a guy line or hank
- `prop-cringle-blank` — 176 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 22 cm oak tray of unfinished cringle blanks: two graduated brass thimbles, a half-worked rope-strand ring and an upright wooden cringle fid
