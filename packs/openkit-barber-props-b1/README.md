# openkit-barber-props-b1

4 medieval-fantasy barber workshop props (CC0, procedural Blender bpy, 1 m units, slots-only FBX).

- `prop-razor-blank-bb1` — 48 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 19 cm unground forged straight-razor blank: dull scaled blade, raw tang, no scales/pin, no edge (workshop blank, not a finished blade; distinct from cutler prop-blade-blank-cu1)
- `prop-bleed-bowl-blank` — 152 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 26 cm shallow unpolished pewter barber's basin blank with broad rim and casting sprue tab (shaving/bleeding bowl form, empty, PG; distinct from wash-basin and soap-dish)
- `prop-hone-stone-blank` — 36 tris — laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 20 cm rectangular slate hone blank with lighter flattened face and unworked quarry chip (distinct from needle-polish-stone and grindstone wheel)
- `prop-soap-ball-tray` — 180 tris — bottom_centre (0,0,0); 24x16 cm shallow board tray holding six hand-rolled shaving-soap balls (spheres, not cut bars/molds; distinct from soap-maker mold tray)
