# openkit-hat-maker-props-b1 (CC0)
Medieval-fantasy milliner / felt-hatter workshop blanks. Not finished wearable hats, helms or icons. Each <=210 tris, 1 m units, Z-up Blender / Y-up FBX. FBX carries material slots only; optional BaseColor PNGs in tex/.

## Pivots
- prop-felt-blank: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 27 cm tall raw wool felt cone (hood) blank standing on its open mouth, pre-blocking, no brim; chalk size tally on -Y face
- prop-hat-block-form: bottom_centre (0,0,0); 19 cm oak hat crown block (milliner's form) standing upright, split-wedge seam line along Y, dowel plug on top
- prop-brim-wire-blank: laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 30 cm two-turn coil of brim stiffener wire lying flat, linen tape binding at +X, loose straight tail to +X/-Y
- prop-hatband-blank: laid / bottom_centre (0,0,0); 3 cm-wide undyed ribbon hatband roll, 9 cm uncut tail laid to +X with chalk cut mark, brass pin by roll
