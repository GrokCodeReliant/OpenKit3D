# Look and limits

- The masonry and timber are chamfered and regular, with little wear, so the court looks cleaner than a real garden.
- The sundial is a stone plinth and shaft under a 10-sided dial plate with incised hour lines and a bronze triangular gnomon. It reads as a sundial from a 50 mm camera at 13 m. Up close the plate is faceted, and the hour lines are simple inlaid strips. This is deliberate: the reference plate box fills the recessed hour lines.
- The birdbath is a stone pedestal with an open shallow bowl and a flat water disc sitting below the rim. The mouth above the water is visually open and is a real collider gap. Up close the bowl is 10-sided, and the water is a flat disc, not a meniscus.
- The planter is a rectangular stone trough with soil and a simple rounded shrub. This is deliberate: one box covers the trough and the soil; there is no opening into the trough. The shrub is a faceted lathed bush; the facets are obvious up close.
- The trellis is a timber frame with a coarse five-by-four lattice and small iron caps on the posts. The openings in the lattice are visually open and are real collider gaps. It still reads as a trellis at 13 m. Up close the slats are simple squared timbers.
- The cold-frame is a low timber box with soil and a sloped lid, slightly open, holding two pale glaze panes. The opening under the raised lid is visually open and is a real collider gap. This is deliberate: the trough box is filled (the soil is a solid fill). Up close the glaze is two flat panes in a simple frame.
- The lavabo is a free-standing stone pedestal basin with a small bronze spout at the back and a flat water disc below the rim. The mouth above the water is visually open and is a real collider gap. It is taller than the birdbath and reads as a basin with a spout from 13 m. Up close the bowl is 10-sided.
- Birdbath and lavabo share the same pedestal and bowl shape; the lavabo reads apart only by its bronze bowl and spout.
- Collision notes describe the reference shells, which are not included.
- An independent GLB parse reports a few unpaired edges on the planter and the cold-frame where the soil meets the walls across material slots; official Blender re-import integrity was closed on every piece, and same-facing coplanar overlaps were 0 on all six.
- All observations above come from Blender Workbench previews, including a 50 mm perspective camera at 13 m. Nothing has been checked under game-engine lighting, against a character, or with a camera-fade shader.
