# openkit-winery-props-b1
4 medieval-fantasy winery props, procedural Blender bpy (src/build.py). 1 m units, Z-up (FBX Y-up), slots-only materials.
## Pivots (all bottom_centre: bbox-centre XY, Z=0 at floor)
- prop-grape-crate — open crate heaped with grape clusters
- prop-wine-press-lever — compact basket press, lever toward +Y
- prop-tasting-bowl — pewter tastevin, thumb tab +X
- prop-cork-basket — wicker basket of cork stoppers
