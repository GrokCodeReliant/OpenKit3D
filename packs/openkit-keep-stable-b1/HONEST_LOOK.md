# Look and limits

- The masonry and the timber are chamfered and regular, with little wear, so the set looks cleaner than a working stable.
- The stall is a kit-sized stone wall with a timber breast about 1.16 m high, a slatted manger pocket on the room face, and an open bay above the breast. It does not open to the floor, so it is not a doorway. It reads as a stall front from a 50 mm camera at 13 m. The built-in manger is a shallow slatted pocket in the breast and is easier to read up close than from far away. This is deliberate: the manger pocket is a recess, not a passage, and the solid breast collider fills it. The upper bay is visually open and is a real collider gap.
- The split door is a kit-sized stone wall with a closed lower timber leaf, iron hinges and a latch, and an open upper leaf. It reads as a stable door from 13 m, not as a full open doorway and not as a cell front. The upper opening is visually open and is a real collider gap. The lower leaf does not swing. Up close the leaf is a set of butted boards with a mid-rail and a diagonal brace.
- The hay hatch is a kit-sized stone wall with a high square opening that does not reach the floor, a timber lining, and a shutter parked to the side on the room face. It reads as a hay hatch from 13 m, not as a window, a door, or a letter box. The opening is a real through-gap. The parked shutter does not swing. Up close the shutter is a few boards and two hinges, slightly inset from the stone face.
- The floor tile is a kit-sized packed-earth field with a stone border and one straight gutter with a small square iron catch. The gutter is a shallow recess with the catch set in the middle. This is deliberate: the solid cell collider fills the joints and the gutter.
- The manger is a free-standing V-shaped slatted box on two posts, with a little hay inside. It reads as a hay manger from 13 m, not as a low feed trough or a washing trough. The mouth above the slats is visually open and is a real collider gap. Up close the hay is a simple block behind the slats, and some of the slats sit close to that block.
- The saddle rack is a standing two-post frame with a top rail and a sloped rest. It is empty, with no saddle on it. It reads as a rack from 13 m, not as a sawhorse or a blank saddle tree.
- The bridle peg rail is a free-standing beam on two posts, with five empty pegs facing the room. It reads as a tack rail from 13 m. Up close the pegs are short cylinders.
- The watering tub is a low round coopered tub with two iron hoops and a flat water disc below the rim. It reads as a drinking tub from 13 m, not as a tall barrel or a long trough. This is deliberate: the mouth is filled by the solid collider. Up close the staves are a twelve-sided lathe, not individual boards.
- The tack bench is a short bench with a lower tray and a low back rail. It reads as a tack bench from 13 m, though a single seat with a back can still recall a settle. The space between the seat and the tray is visually open and is a real collider gap.
- The blanket rail is two posts with a high bar and a lower bar, empty, with iron ferrules on the post tops. It reads as a rail from 13 m. A two-bar timber frame can still recall a simple fence panel; the height and the empty bars are what mark it as a hanging rail.
- Collision notes describe the reference shells, which are not included.
- An independent parse of the glTF export (not included in this pack) reports coplanar same-facing overlaps on the three walls, the floor tile, the manger, the peg rail and the blanket rail, most of them on the coursed masonry of the walls, and unpaired edges on the stall, the split door, the hay hatch, the saddle rack and the floor tile where parts of different material slots meet. Official Blender re-import integrity was closed on every piece.
- All observations above come from Blender Workbench previews, including a 50 mm perspective camera at 13 m, except the engine-test note that follows. These files passed the Open Kit team's Unity engine test on 25 Nov 2026 (Unity 6000.4.0f1). Results apply to that version and those settings; other engines, Unity versions or import settings may differ. The room side measured at Unity +Z. See the README Engine test section for what was run and what was not. Nothing in this pack has been checked under game-engine lighting, or with an on-screen camera-fade shader.
