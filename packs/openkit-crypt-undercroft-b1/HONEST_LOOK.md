# Look and limits

- Stone blocks and slabs have chamfered edges like the kit's own modules. There is little wear or chipping, so the masonry looks cleaner and more regular than a real crypt wall.
- The wall fountain is a recess cut into a standard 1.5 × 3.0 × 0.34 m wall, not a projecting bowl. The niche, basin and dark water still read from a 50 mm camera at 13 m. Collision is two solid boxes for the whole wall (body and cap); the bowl is filled, so nothing can sit in it. Say this on purpose: it is a panel, not a hollow basin.
- The cistern is an open indoor tank, not a well (no crank, no raised parapet, no bucket). Dark water sits 0.30 m below the rim and still reads at 13 m. Collision follows the walls and the floor up to the water plane; the mouth above the water is a real opening.
- The urn is a closed, lidded vessel on a plinth. It does not open and has no remains. Up close the body is a simple lathed silhouette.
- The ossuary cist is a closed stone box with iron straps and four short feet. No bones are modelled. The space between the legs is a real collider gap, not a single envelope around the whole chest.
- The memorial floor tile carries a 2.2 cm recessed field and a bronze rosette whose top is 8 mm below the walking surface, so the carving stays under the floor-top pivot cap and still reads from 13 m (a dark square and a rosette on the front-left cell of the 13 m layout). Collision fills the recesses; you walk over them as if they were flush.
- The sluice is a small timber frame with a raised iron plate and a clear opening under it. The gap is a real collider opening and is visually open (you can see through it). It is not a keep portcullis.
- An independent GLB parse (not the official Blender validator) still reports coplanar same-facing overlaps where separate parts meet, and unpaired edges on the memorial tile after vertex welding. Official Blender re-import integrity and kit-snap gates passed.
- All observations above come from Blender Workbench previews, including a 50 mm perspective camera at 13 m. Nothing has been checked under game-engine lighting, against a character, or with a camera-fade shader.
